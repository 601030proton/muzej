Experimental Music in Macedonia

Independent radio, record labels, and ten recordings from the mid-2020s.

Experimental music in Macedonia includes post-punk, ambient and electronic composition, and free improvisation. In Skopje, Kanal 103 has broadcast independent music since 1991 and releases records under the name Stotrojka. Other labels represented here include PMG Jazz, Sharla, and Mrtov Konj, which the Gola Planina collective established in 2024.

Chlen 16 · Mrtov Konj

Нејќам веќе ваков мир

Chlen 16 released this single on July 10, 2026. Luka Jankulovski plays synthesizer and sings, Marko Pečenović plays bass and sings, and Blagojče Aceski plays drums. Rade Bikovski recorded, mixed, and mastered the single at Studio Alshar. The title translates as “I don’t want this kind of peace anymore.”

Radio and electronic music

Kanal 103 began broadcasting in Skopje in 1991 as an experimental youth station within Macedonian Radio. It moved its broadcasts online after losing its FM frequency in 2019. On its thirtieth anniversary in 2021, the station suspended its online stream for 24 hours to protest the loss of the frequency.

Fydhws · Stotrojka

oд почеток?

Fydhws is the solo project of Kristijan Petreski. Released by Stotrojka in May 2026, од почеток? is the soundtrack to his short film of the same name, which he assembled from footage shot in Prague.

Toni Dimitrov · Kirigirisu Recordings

Ghosts of Atlantis

Released by Kirigirisu Recordings, the album contains two compositions of 28 minutes and 16 seconds each. Both use sustained tones and intermittent noise. The pieces are titled “Ghosts of Atlantis” and “African Temples.”

Open on Bandcamp

Lazar Darkovski · Elektramusic

Egon

Darkovski is a musician and visual artist based in Skopje. Egon contains seven compositions for piano and electronics. “Bel um” is the opening track.

Open on Bandcamp

Gola Planina and Mrtov Konj

Gola Planina and Umnik released a compilation of Macedonian independent music drawn from 2022. It opens with Vagina Corporation’s “Германски рок.” Gola Planina also organized the traveling festival Sepak Se Vrti with local groups including Dona in Gostivar, Boiling Point in Kumanovo, and Goongula in Prilep.

Mrtov Konj’s catalog includes Chlen 16, Lea Carine, Alembic, and Pondskull. Rade Bikovski recorded Chlen 16’s single and mixed and mastered Lea Carine’s debut. Gjorgji Janevski contributed field recordings to the latter.

Lea Carine · Mrtov Konj

Lea Carine

The self-titled debut by Andrej Dimovski and Aleksandra Arsovska contains eight tracks. Dimovski is credited with instrumentation and vocals and Arsovska with vocals and lyrics. The recordings include guitar, drums, spoken passages, and field recordings.

Open on Bandcamp

Pondskull · Mrtov Konj

Pondskull

Pondskull is the solo project of Mae. The self-titled release contains two tracks. The second documents a performance by the Pondskull Interdisciplinary Musical Ensemble at Social Center Dunja in Skopje on January 23, 2026.

Open on Bandcamp

Alembic · Self-released

Без никого да удриш

Alembic released its second studio album on February 1, 2026. Iva Damjanovski and Viktor Tanaskovski wrote the music, with Filip Efinski co-writing three tracks. Damjanovski is credited with vocals, synthesizer, and theremin, and Dragan Teodosiev with drums.

Open on Bandcamp

Improvisation and vocal music

Guitarist Filip Bukrshliev appears on both the PMG Jazz Sveti Nikole Session series and the debut album by Svirci Vardarci, released by Sharla. Kristo Rodzevski’s Orthodoxia Kai Thanatos is a separate project based on Orthodox chant.

Blagojche Tomevski, Filip Bukrshliev & Dorjan Jovanovikj · PMG Jazz

Sveti Nikole Session Vol. 1

The first volume consists of “Orel,” recorded at the Krste Misirkov cultural center in Sveti Nikole on October 6, 2024. Blagojche Tomevski plays clarinets and flutes, Filip Bukrshliev plays guitar, and Dorjan Jovanovikj plays Chapman Stick and electronics.

Open on Bandcamp

Svirci Vardarci · Sharla

Svirci Vardarci LP

Svirci Vardarci is a Skopje trio comprising Yordan Kostov on accordion and synthesizer, Filip Bukrshliev on electric guitar, and Tejka Vasiljevic on drums. The four-track debut was recorded at Studio Alshar. Andreas Günther appears on the opening track in place of Vasiljevic.

Open on Bandcamp

Kristo Rodzevski · Port of Entry

Orthodoxia Kai Thanatos

Rodzevski, a vocalist and composer from Bitola, recorded a 26-track album based on chants from several Christian Orthodox traditions. The first 13 tracks feature his voice alone. The second half includes electronics by collaborators including Fennesz, Ikue Mori, Bill Laswell, and Thomas Fehlmann.
