The Nation on Their Backs

1994 1998 2016 2021 2023

Gems · 1994

Polo collar

The 1994 Gems home shirt has a yellow polo collar and buttoned placket. The red body is made of jacquard fabric with a subtle woven pattern.

Puma · 1998

Chest band

The 1998 Puma home shirt uses a broad yellow band edged in white across the chest and sleeves. The band breaks at the center for the Puma logo. The cuffs are yellow, and the red collar has yellow-and-white trim.

JAKO · 2016

Rays from the crest

JAKO’s 2016 home shirt has yellow rays extending across the red front from the federation crest.

Macedonia qualified for its first European Championship in November 2020 wearing the 2016 design. In its qualification announcement, JAKO associated the sun shirt with the campaign and announced a new kit for the tournament.

JAKO · 2021

Tone-on-tone lynx

Withdrawn before Euro 2020

In May 2021, ahead of Macedonia’s European Championship debut at Euro 2020, the football federation unveiled a burgundy home shirt with an embossed geometric lynx. Within days, the federation withdrew the design and announced that the team would wear its existing red-and-yellow sun kit.

Contemporary reporting documented a petition to retain the previous shirt. Supporters objected to the darker red, the reduced use of yellow, and the replacement of the sun with a lynx. Footballer Natasha Andonova publicly defended the use of red and yellow.

JAKO · 2023

Sun around the player number

The 2016 sun design remained in use until JAKO introduced a new home kit in 2023.

The 2023 home shirt retained the sun motif. Short yellow and orange strokes form rays around an open red center positioned for the player’s number. The sleeves have yellow cuffs.
