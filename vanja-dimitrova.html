Nowhere Somewhere

From the exhibition text supplied by Vanja Dimitrova

Nowhere Somewhere is an exhibition featuring cinematic photography by Vanja Dimitrova. The exhibition blends photography and narrative, encouraging viewers to reimagine the artworks through a new lens, where each image unfolds a new story.

Captured in a dreamlike surrealist style, Dimitrova’s subjects appear simultaneously anchored and adrift, caught in an undefined somewhere while conceptually hinting at a possible nowhere, thus emphasizing the inherent ambiguity of time, place, and story.

Curator Natali Rajcinovska Pavleska defines Dimitrova’s work as a “deconstructed film book” encouraging prolonged reflection and seeing reality through this specific cinematic lens.

The characters in the photographs are from diverse nationalities: Irish, Dutch, Macedonian, Bosnian, German, Australian, Ukrainian, Russian and Portuguese, and were captured in various locations across the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and Macedonia.
