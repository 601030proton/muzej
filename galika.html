Galika

Words Aleksandar Trajkovski

An electronic music festival in Galičnik.

Bare Minimum · BM001

Sasho Uzun — Da Funk

Released October 20, 2025.
Artwork David Manev · Mastering RV Audio.

Open in Bandcamp ↗

Galika is an electronic music festival organized by Skopje record label Bare Minimum. Its first edition took place at Sherpa Horse Ranch in Galičnik on August 1, 2026, with house and tech house from 6 p.m. to 6 a.m.

The lineup included Aceedo & Sasho Uzun, BASHKKA, Ermilova, MADAMI, Mongo & Omaru, and Soul Case.

Uzun’s Da Funk was Bare Minimum’s first release. The four-track EP came out on October 20, 2025, with artwork by David Manev and mastering by RV Audio.
