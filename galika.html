Galika

Words Aleksandar Trajkovski

A Macedonian record label takes its dance floor into the mountains.

At Galika, the address is part of the appeal: a horse ranch in Galičnik, with an electronic music program running from evening into the following morning. Organized by the Macedonian label Bare Minimum, the first edition brought BASHKKA onto a bill with Aceedo & Sasho Uzun, Emi Ermilova, MADAMI, Mongo & Omaru, and Soul Case.

The August 1, 2026 program set out a twelve-hour night at Sherpa Horse Ranch, with MADAMI billed live and sound supplied by Phonolux through a d&b audiotechnik system. The capacity was limited. The setting was expansive; the format kept the gathering to one night.

Behind the event is a smaller, more sustained story about people putting music into circulation. Bare Minimum began its catalog with Sasho Uzun’s Da Funk, released in October 2025. Four tracks, artwork by David Manev, mastering by RV Audio: a first record with its own visual identity and a direct connection to Skopje’s clubs.

Uzun worked at Sektor 909 as both a resident DJ and booking manager. Those jobs put him in charge of different parts of the same exchange: the records being played and the people invited to play them. The label identifies the club as an influence on the EP.

A release party followed at Klub1212 in November 2025. Aceedo, Uzun, and Soul Case shared that bill, then appeared together again at Galika. The project had a record and a set of working relationships before it had a mountain festival.

Ermilova’s involvement reaches into another part of that history. She began playing house music in 2008, according to a British Council biography published a decade later. In 2018 she was already a co-owner and resident of Longbus, a collective organizing parties in different locations.

Her Refuge Worldwide profile offers a good detail about how that musical life formed: her father’s records from the 1980s and 1990s have effectively become hers, too. Alongside house and electro, the profile names ambient, new wave, disco, post-punk, and dub. A festival lineup can be read as a list of genres; it can also be read as a meeting of record collections.

Soul Case arrived at dance music through sample-based hip-hop, soul, and disco. His biography dates the formation of Oubli, a project he founded with two friends, to winter 2018. Across these artists, playing music and making opportunities to play it have developed together.

BASHKKA brought a different set of connections. Born in Munich, she spent a decade in New York, where Brooklyn’s trans community helped shape her life and work. Her practice has taken her through BLITZ and Panorama Bar; her 2024 Resident Advisor interview discusses the people and dance floors behind that trajectory.

Galika’s own announcement placed welcome and self-expression alongside its musical program, with an explicit policy against discrimination and harmful behavior. That promise belongs to the practical work of running a party, as much as booking the DJs or choosing the sound system.

The festival’s name comes with a character in its promotional writing: a mare, a village girl, a fairy. The announcement invokes Ilinden and imagines a gathering bound to its surroundings. It gives the project a local identity while leaving the musicians room to bring their own references.

For a listener discovering this part of Macedonia, there are several ways in: a DJ whose collection starts with her father’s records, a producer shaped by Sektor 909, or a label’s first four tracks. Galika puts them on the same bill, at an address worth remembering.
