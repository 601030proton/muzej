Coffee

Tigre

Deveani 13

Map →

Start with an espresso at the roastery. The café hosted the opening press conference for Bitola Open City in June 2026.

Breakfast

Fresh

Petar Petrović Njegoš 13

Map →

Get a piece of burek and a yogurt at this no-frills neighborhood bakery. Eat it warm before heading for coffee.

Lunch

Pionerski Book Bar

Ruzveltova 20

Map →

Bring a paperback to the book bar on Ruzveltova. Order lunch, read a few pages and resist the temptation to schedule the rest of the afternoon.

Art bar

Radost

Fushtani

Map →

Off Širok Sokak, Radost is a small art café with a book corner and occasional music events. Check what’s happening before you go.

Cinema

Kino Manaki

Širok Sokak

Map →

Check the screening program. In September, look out for the Manaki Brothers cinematography festival.

Records

Jagoda

Bitola

Map →

The café hosted the festival’s vinyl exchange and DJ Chiko in both 2025 and 2026. Look for the next record fair.

Late-night food

Bombata

Šetalište

Map →

A Bitola fast-food institution on the promenade. Get a burger or toastie after a gig rather than making dinner an occasion.

Music

Porta Jazz

Petar Petrović Njegoš 13

Map →

Check the gig listings. In 2025, electronic musician Herzel led a DJ workshop here as part of Bitola Open City.

Two ways to spend the weekend

Bitola Open City weekend

The 25th edition took place June 24–27, 2026. Check the organizer for future dates.

Organizer →

Friday

Follow the music

Start at Tigre and follow the festival program into the evening.

Saturday

Records and guitars

Look for the vinyl exchange at Jagoda and the skatepark concerts on the festival program.

Any other weekend

Saturday

Coffee and cinema

Pick up burek at Fresh, take your time over coffee at Tigre and see what’s playing at Kino Manaki. Finish at Porta Jazz, then get a toastie at Bombata.

Sunday

No plans

Take a long coffee at Tigre, walk along the Dragor and stop at Radost.

Follow the scene

AKTO

Founded in Bitola in 2006, AKTO is a contemporary arts festival with an emphasis on interdisciplinary and critical practice. Follow its program for exhibitions, performances and discussions rather than relying on a fixed gallery itinerary.
