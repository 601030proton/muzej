Experimental

Downstairs at Kotur

Centar

Mono-Ton listings →

Kanal 103 held an open electronic improvisation session here. On the recording, you’ll hear Martin Djorlev join Aleksandar Ordev near the end, playing a Roland SH-101. The basement has also hosted coldwave and industrial techno on the same bill.

Igroteka · Kanal 103 / Radia

Party series

BURU

Location varies

Past BURU lineup →

BURU has put a dance floor in both the Museum of Contemporary Art and the old railway station that houses the city museum. Look for residents Rabo de Toro and check the address on the flyer.

Club

Minus Eden

Karpoš

Club listings →

A small club in Karpoš for house and techno after midnight.

DIY gigs

Dunja

Rajko Žinzifov 22

Mono-Ton listings →

Dunja is a social center with a small courtyard. Its music program ranges from improvised sets to hardcore shows. Some gigs are by donation.

Late food

Silbo

Majakovski 1–4/1

Map →

Get cheese burek. If there’s vrtena pita left, get a piece of that too. Check the bakery’s hours if you’re heading out late Saturday.
