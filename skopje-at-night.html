Weekend Guide

A Local’s Guide to Skopje at Night

Wine on Ruzveltova, a basement set at Kotur, and burek on the way home.

One night

El Cabron → Kotur → Minus Eden → Silbo. If BURU is on, make it the main event.

Wine

El Cabron

Ruzveltova 63A

Wine list →

Ask what Macedonian wine is open and try a glass of Vranec if they’re pouring one. Look for their DJ nights. DJ Ikac has played house and jazz here alongside a tasting.

Dinner

Destan

Old Bazaar

Map →

Get a plate of kebapi with bread and onions. This is the quick dinner for when you still want to make the opening set.

Experimental

Downstairs at Kotur

Centar

Mono-Ton listings →

Kanal 103 held an open electronic improvisation session here. On the recording, you’ll hear Martin Djorlev join Aleksandar Ordev near the end, playing a Roland SH-101. The basement has also hosted coldwave and industrial techno on the same bill.

Igroteka · Kanal 103 / Radia

Party series

BURU

Location varies

BURU on Resident Advisor →

If BURU is on, build your night around it. The collective has put a dance floor in both the Museum of Contemporary Art and the old railway station that houses the city museum. Look for residents Rabo de Toro and check the address on the flyer.

Club

Minus Eden

Karpoš

Club listings →

Go for house and techno after midnight. Look for the promoter as well as the DJ. The club has also hosted Radosnica, a queer party curated by Butchkuruzh with performances by local drag kings.

DIY gigs

Dunja

Rajko Žinzifov 22

Mono-Ton listings →

If you’d rather be in a pit, check what’s on at Dunja. One recent bill paired Skopje post-hardcore band Sokoli with FOA, with a suggested donation of 200 denars.

Late food

Silbo

Majakovski 1–4/1

Map →

Get cheese burek. If there’s vrtena pita left, get a piece of that too. Check the bakery’s hours if you’re heading out late Saturday.
