Sound | Ritual Song

Dodole

Field Recording

Istibanja, Macedonia

Listen on YouTube

Dodole is a ritual song associated with requests for rain and the protection of crops. Its text invokes God through a recurring refrain, linking rainfall to the watering of fields and the agricultural yield.

The practice varied by locality. In her study of Gorna Reka, ethnologist Mirjana Mirchevska examines Dodole as a rite performed by designated participants on behalf of the village community. Documentation of the Istibanja repertoire in Les voix du monde associates these songs with processions of women and girls.

The anthology describes a performance by three women, with a lead voice and two singers sustaining a unison drone. The voices converge at the end of each couplet. This description concerns the published Istibanja recording; the precise relationship between that recording and the audio presented here has not been established.

The song

Дудулева мајка крај море седи
Вај, дудуле, дај Боже дожд

Dudule’s mother sits by the sea.
O Dudule, God, grant us rain.

Крај море седи, Бога си моли
Вај, дудуле, дај Боже дожд

She sits by the sea and prays to God.
O Dudule, God, grant us rain.

Да си зароси таја ситна роса
Вај, дудуле, дај Боже дожд

Let that fine dew fall.
O Dudule, God, grant us rain.

Да си навади по полето
Вај, дудуле, дај Боже дожд

Let it water the fields.
O Dudule, God, grant us rain.

Ај по полето бериќето
Вај, дудуле, дај Боже дожд

Ah, let the fields yield a harvest.
O Dudule, God, grant us rain.

Recording & sources

Audio presented through Muzej’s YouTube channel. A separate upload, published September 10, 2021, attributes the recording to Bernard Lortat-Jacob and dates it to 1987. That description does not name the performers.

The Muséum national d’Histoire naturelle’s listening guide instead credits an Istibanja Dodole recording to Bernard Lortat-Jacob and Jacques Bouët, 1990, issued on Les voix du monde (1996), disc III, track 15. Its anthology notes describe the vocal arrangement above. The correspondence between the audio presented here and this archival entry remains unconfirmed; 1987 is therefore not treated as a verified recording date.

Ritual context: Mirjana Mirchevska, The Villages in the Ethnographic Region of Gorna Reka as Religious-Ritual Communities.
