Born in Skopje in 1974, Ivanoska works across drawing, textiles, performance, installation and public space. With Yane Calovski, she co-founded Press to Exit Project Space in 2004.

Artist’s website

Selected works

The photograph of Ivanoska holding a rifle comes from Document Missing: Performance no. 5 (Three Actions), a 2017 HD video lasting five minutes and 45 seconds. Ivanoska performs the role of Rosa Plaveva, a Macedonian revolutionary and women’s rights activist. The catalogue for Precarity Has a Chance describes the film’s three scenes as historically possible actions by Plaveva in 1903. Yane Calovski made the video and photographs; Gjorgje Jovanovik edited the film.

Three Actions belongs to Document Missing, the series Ivanoska began in 2014. It includes live performances, video, scripts, garments, and installations based on her research into women’s political activity in Macedonia.

Freedom, Emancipation, Education, Labour!

For the Museum of Yugoslavia in Belgrade, Ivanoska made a 14.3-by-3.5-meter mural in 2024. It reworks Đorđe Andrejević Kun’s 1957 mosaic in Ivanjica through shapes derived from Cyrillic letters. The lettering uses her typeface, Archetype Open Form. The commission marked the 120th anniversary of Kun’s birth.

Easy Accumulation of Capital

Presented at Skopje’s Museum of Contemporary Art in 2019, this project addresses working conditions in Macedonia’s textile industry. It includes embroidery, a mural, and a services contract between Ivanoska and the museum. The contract specifies the conditions under which she carried out the work and is displayed as part of the installation.

The title refers to Rosa Luxemburg’s The Accumulation of Capital. A subsequent version was included in Art at Work at the Museum of Contemporary Art Metelkova in Ljubljana in 2022.

Naming the Bridge

In 2004, Ivanoska proposed naming a new Skopje bridge after Rosa Plaveva and Nakie Bajram. The project includes public proposals, printed material, and photographic documentation. In 2021, she returned to the proposal through Women for Women, co-produced by Tiiiit! Inc.

What Cannot Be Closed Within Itself

The 2026 installation at Skopje’s Museum of Contemporary Art combines seven layered paper-cut drawings with a dry-pigment mural measuring six by two meters. Three drawings measure 70 by 50 centimeters; four are A4. Words in Archetype Open Form are incorporated into the cut-paper compositions. The work was commissioned for Anarchic Bodies: Consent Not to Be a Single Being, curated by Slavcho Dimitrov for Skopje Pride Weekend.
