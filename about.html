A journal of
Macedonian culture.

Muzej publishes essays, conversations, and field guides on art, architecture, and everyday life—from Vardar, Pirin, and Egej to Macedonian communities around the world.

An independent journal published in English, Muzej was founded and is edited by Aleksandar Trajkovski, a designer and artist from the Macedonian diaspora.
